# Code of Conduct

## Our promise

Eddyter's community includes developers and businesses from many countries, cultures and backgrounds. We want everyone to feel welcome, respected and safe when they take part, whatever their experience level, identity, language or location.

This Code of Conduct applies in every Eddyter space: GitHub repositories, issues, pull requests, Discussions, our Discord server, social media channels and events. It also applies when someone represents Eddyter in public.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new or who write in a second language.
- Assume good intent, and ask questions before drawing conclusions.
- Give feedback on ideas and code, not on people.
- Accept constructive criticism with an open mind.
- Respect people's time: search before asking, and give clear details.
- Credit other people's work.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks
- Discriminatory jokes, language or imagery
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwanted sexual attention
- Trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or repeated off-topic posts
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission
- Spam, including unsolicited advertising
- Any other behavior that would be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

The Eddyter team is responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct. We may edit or remove comments, code, issues and other contributions that break it, and we may temporarily or permanently ban anyone from Eddyter spaces.

Depending on the situation, we may:

1. **Remind** someone privately of the expected behavior.
2. **Warn** them, with a clear explanation of what must change.
3. **Suspend** them temporarily from Eddyter spaces.
4. **Ban** them permanently, for serious or repeated violations.
